# S2.6 / c32.ideate - Original-thinking engine

## Source pin
Verbatim excerpt: "able to think originally (train models specifically for this purpose)", "merge cool and weird ideas together in a similar way to how humans brainstorm", "make fiction into reality".

## What it does
Combinatorial recombination of SUPPLIED concepts (pairs x six SCAMPER-style ops), novelty scored as 1 - jaccard vs a supplied prior-art index, ranked deterministically. Training-data export is a record.

## What it does not do
No model training (compute purchase: TREND_REFUSED_SPEND via c30.policy). "Fiction into reality" is not claimed.

## Refusals
TREND_REFUSED_SPEND, TREND_REFUSED_TOO_FEW:concepts, TREND_REFUSED_OVERSIZE, TREND_REFUSED_BAD_TYPE:*, TREND_REFUSED_EMPTY:concept, TREND_REFUSED_CONTROL_CHARS:*, TREND_REFUSED_TEXT_TOO_LONG:*.

## Limits
MAX_CONCEPTS = 64 (2016 pairs x 6 ops); MAX_PRIOR_ART = 4096. IC: op set, novelty metric, ranking order and caps are interpretation choices.
