systemizing, melancholic-depth, lateral-jump, constraint-flip) to a supplied

diagnoses or imitations of any condition.

CONTROL_CHARS:idea / TEXT_TOO_LONG:idea / BAD_LENS:<lens> / DUPLICATE:<lens>.
