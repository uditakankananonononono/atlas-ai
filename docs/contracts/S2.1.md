# S2.1 / c33.self_feed - Feed-and-follow analyzer

## Source pin
Verbatim excerpt: "analyse my feed across social media", "see my likes and comments on social media", "see the people I follow and try to create similar work/products they have built but better".

## What it does
Parses her SUPPLIED official data exports or pasted lists (liked posts, follows); clusters by a fixed theme taxonomy; emits inspiration briefs as drafts ("similar-but-better" framing per her line).

## What it does not do
Never reads any account directly. Third-party private data refused (SOCIAL_REFUSED_THIRDPARTYPRIVATE). Briefs are original drafts, never copies of followed accounts' work.

## Refusals
SOCIAL_REFUSED_NOEXPORT, SOCIAL_REFUSED_OVERSIZE, SOCIAL_REFUSED_THIRDPARTYPRIVATE, SOCIAL_REFUSED_BAD_TYPE:*, SOCIAL_REFUSED_EMPTY:*, SOCIAL_REFUSED_CONTROL_CHARS:*, SOCIAL_REFUSED_TEXT_TOO_LONG:*.

## Limits
MAX_ITEMS = 100000 per list; six-theme taxonomy. IC: taxonomy words, brief template, top-theme ordering and the private-flag trigger are interpretation choices.
