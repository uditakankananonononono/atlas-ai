# S2.5 / c32.image_brief - Image-concept generator

## Source pin
Verbatim excerpt: "generate some highly realistic images of this with AI making it more girly".

## What it does
Prompt briefs and style boards for her chosen image tool over SUPPLIED concepts. Every output is labeled AI-generated; deceptive realism refused (TREND_REFUSED_DECEPTIVE).

## What it does not do
Generates no images itself. No deceptive realism claims in marketing. No tool signup or spend.

## Refusals
TREND_REFUSED_DECEPTIVE, TREND_REFUSED_BAD_STYLE:<value>, TREND_REFUSED_BAD_TYPE:*, TREND_REFUSED_EMPTY:*, TREND_REFUSED_CONTROL_CHARS:*, TREND_REFUSED_TEXT_TOO_LONG:*.

## Limits
Four styles (soft-pastel, glossy-editorial, cozy-handmade, bold-pop); subject <= 256 chars. IC: style vocabulary, prompt template and the two deceptive flags (claim_real, market_as_real) are interpretation choices.
