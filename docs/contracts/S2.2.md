# S2.2 / c33.corpus - Public-post corpus analyzer

## Source pin
Verbatim excerpt: "go through all posts ever on social media and analyse them... that's where you'll learn how humans are like".

## What it does
Topic, sentiment, emotion and virality features over a SUPPLIED public research dataset. Corpus size is computed and reported honestly. Substitute for X2.

## What it does not do
No scraping or bulk platform access; no claim of "all posts ever". Features are lexicon counts, not measurement of humans.

## Refusals
SOCIAL_REFUSED_BAD_TYPE:*, SOCIAL_REFUSED_EMPTY:posts, SOCIAL_REFUSED_OVERSIZE, SOCIAL_REFUSED_CONTROL_CHARS:*, SOCIAL_REFUSED_TEXT_TOO_LONG:*.

## Limits
MAX_POSTS = 100000; engagement counters non-negative ints. IC: topic/sentiment/emotion lexicons, sentiment rule (pos vs neg count), virality = likes + shares, avg_virality rounding.
