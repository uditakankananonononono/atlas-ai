# S2.9 / c32.psyche - Emotion-psychology model

## Source pin
Verbatim excerpt: "think with human emotions all, physchology and all way human to the bone".

## What it does
Lexicon-and-rule emotion (six classes) and motive (three classes) tagging over SUPPLIED text, with dominant-emotion pick and a mandatory validity_limits field. claims_feeling is always False.

## What it does not do
It does not feel, and does not measure any person's actual emotions or motives; no clinical or diagnostic claims.

## Refusals
TREND_REFUSED_BAD_TYPE:text, TREND_REFUSED_EMPTY:text, TREND_REFUSED_CONTROL_CHARS:text, TREND_REFUSED_TEXT_TOO_LONG:text.

## Limits
Text <= 200000 chars; closed lexicons. IC: emotion/motive lexicons, dominance rule (highest count, name tiebreak) and the validity_limits wording are interpretation choices.
