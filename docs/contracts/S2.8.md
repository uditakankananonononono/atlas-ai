# S2.8 / c32.thought - Human-thought corpus

## Source pin
Verbatim excerpt: "Analyse intelligent human thinking process across media from podcasts, books, audio, music etc all forms".

## What it does
Reasoning-move tagging (analogy, inversion, first_principles, narrative, heuristic, systems) over SUPPLIED transcripts, text and lyrics; per-doc tags and corpus totals, docs ranked by move total.

## What it does not do
Audio is not transcribed offline (no bundled ASR): audio entries are refused (TREND_REFUSED_AUDIO) and the limit is recorded in scope.not_implemented. "All forms" of media is not claimed.

## Refusals
TREND_REFUSED_AUDIO, TREND_REFUSED_BAD_KIND:<value>, TREND_REFUSED_EMPTY:texts, TREND_REFUSED_OVERSIZE, TREND_REFUSED_BAD_TYPE:*, TREND_REFUSED_CONTROL_CHARS:*, TREND_REFUSED_TEXT_TOO_LONG:*.

## Limits
MAX_TEXTS = 10000; six move classes. IC: move lexicons, kind vocabulary and the offline audio refusal are interpretation choices (the spec's local-ASR note needs a model download, which is out of sandbox scope).
