## Delta against the existing mechanism
Existing: `backend/app/modules/m20_general_cognitive_worker/engineering_1510_1559.py:193` `_taguchi` (read) and its test `tests/modules/test_m20_engineering_1510_1559.py:62`. `_taguchi` takes a flat `responses` list through `_values` (L28, float casts), a `goal` defaulting to larger, and returns one float signal-to-noise ratio (larger: `-10*log10(mean(1/x^2))`, smaller: `-10*log10(mean(x^2))`, nominal: `10*log10(mean^2/pstdev^2)`, with infinity when the deviation is zero), the responses and the `orthogonal_array` value passed through unexamined (default None). It has no runs, no factors, no level check, no orthogonality check, no per-factor summary, no number grammar, no count bounds, no refusal codes; the larger goal divides by zero for a zero response and nan or negative inputs are not refused beyond finiteness.
This unit (`taguchi_methods1540.py`, `analyze_taguchi_runs`, new file; it does not import or modify the existing one) differs in these checkable ways:
- Input is runs (id, factor levels, responses) plus the declared factor names; the array is examined, not passed through.
- Responses are decimal strings in the grammar of the last child section below (ASCII, no exponent, no leading zeros, at most 80 characters, 64 significant digits, 64 fraction digits, absolute value at most 10^12); floats, ints, bools and padded strings are refused. The means and variance are exact rational numbers; the logarithm is evaluated with the decimal module.
- `goal` is required: larger needs every response above zero; smaller needs a nonzero mean square; nominal needs at least two responses, a nonzero mean and a nonzero population variance (the existing formula, using the population deviation). Degenerate cases are refused, not returned as infinity.
- Per run: signal-to-noise in dB. Per factor: observed levels, a balance flag (every observed level used equally often) and the mean signal-to-noise per level (sum of the rounded run values divided by the count, rounded half-even). The means are a reading of the supplied numbers only; no output field names a level.
- A declared array is accepted only if every factor is balanced and, for every pair of factors, every combination of observed levels appears equally often; otherwise it is refused as `TAGUCH1540:not_orthogonal`. A single factor is accepted if balanced.
- Refusals are `ValueError("TAGUCH1540:<code>")` with fixed codes that never echo input.

## Input
Exactly `goal` (larger, smaller or nominal), `factors` (1 to 8 unique names) and `runs`. Each run is exactly `id` (unique), `levels` (a list of integers 1 to 9, one per factor, in factor order) and `responses` (1 to 16 decimal strings). Runs: 1 to 64. Text (factor names, ids) is nonblank, at most 128 characters, no control characters. Input is not mutated and output holds no aliases.

## Refusal codes
invalid_packet, unknown_fields:packet, invalid_goal, invalid_count:factors, invalid_text:factor, duplicate_factor, invalid_count:runs, invalid_record:runs, invalid_fields:runs, invalid_text:id, duplicate_id, invalid_levels, invalid_count:responses, invalid_number, number_too_long, number_precision, number_range, undefined_sn, not_orthogonal, arithmetic. All prefixed `TAGUCH1540:`.

## Limits
Bookkeeping over declared runs only. No array generation or lookup (the caller declares the array; a named array such as L8 is not checked against a catalogue), no interaction analysis, no ANOVA, no confirmation run, no loss function, no optimization, no robustness certification, no physical test or result. Responses and levels are declared by the caller and not verified. Not an acceptance or closure claim.

## Text characters (child update)
Text fields refuse any character that is a Unicode control, line or paragraph separator, or bidi or format character: categories Cc (including DEL, U+0085), Zl (U+2028), Zp (U+2029) and the ranges U+200B-U+200F, U+202A-U+202E, U+2066-U+2069 and U+FEFF. In short: no Unicode control, line/paragraph separator or bidi/format characters. Ordinary spaces and printable non-ASCII text stay allowed. The helper is `_hidden`, applied inside `_text` after the type, length cap and blank checks.

## Child update: final author spec (peer-stated, relayed; not owner words)
- Caps are checked before anything is parsed: factors 8, runs 64, responses per run 16, each refused as `invalid_count:factors|runs|responses`; levels are integers 1 to 9 (`invalid_levels`). Ids, levels and factor names: text at most 128 characters with the Unicode refusal above.
- Array: declared by the caller and checked for pairwise balance only: every two-factor pair of observed levels must occur equally often, else the packet is refused as `TAGUCH1540:not_orthogonal` (a non-orthogonal array is never returned as an output value; peer-stated rule, relayed). `array_status` is always `pairwise_balanced` in a returned result. It is not matched to any standard or catalogue array (L4, L8, L9 and so on) and no standard or Taguchi array name is claimed; the output carries `array_note` saying so. `orthogonal_pairwise` and the field that named a level from the means were removed.
- Per factor-level mean signal-to-noise is bookkeeping only. No claim about a level, an ordering of levels, a recommendation or a design quality appears anywhere in the output (scan pin in the tests).
- Response grammar (this unit's reading of "same grammar as 1536", which I have not read): optional leading minus, ASCII digits with an optional fraction, no exponent, plus sign, spaces, NaN or Infinity; at most 80 characters, at most 64 significant digits and at most 64 fraction digits (the peer-stated 1536 reading as relayed by Main). Refusal `invalid_number:responses`. This replaces the earlier 12.6-digit grammar.
- Decimal context: explicit local `Context(prec=60, rounding=ROUND_HALF_EVEN)` with Overflow, Underflow, InvalidOperation and DivisionByZero trapped and mapped to `TAGUCH1540:arithmetic`; results quantized to 6 places half-even. All arithmetic, including the level means, uses that context, so the ambient context cannot change results (pins at ambient precision 2, 1 and 100 and ROUND_DOWN, ROUND_UP, ROUND_HALF_UP). Emin is -999999 and Emax is 999999 (peer-stated numbers, relayed by Main). Inputs of up to 64 significant digits are parsed exactly into rational numbers and are never rounded, so they need not fit the 60-digit context; that context applies only to the derived logarithms, means and signal-to-noise values.
- Undefined cases (log of zero or a negative number, division by zero, zero variance, a nominal form with too few responses or a zero mean) are refused as `TAGUCH1540:undefined_sn`; no infinity and no silent zero. `goal` is required with no default. The refusals `response_not_positive` and `degenerate_responses` were replaced by `undefined_sn`.
- Prefix stays `TAGUCH1540:`.

## Child update: number grammar, output words, mean arithmetic (peer-stated, relayed; not owner words)
This section supersedes the earlier response-grammar bullet and its `invalid_number:responses` code where they differ.
- Response grammar: ASCII `fullmatch` of `(-?)(0|[1-9][0-9]*)(\.[0-9]+)?`. Legal: `0`, `-0`, `0.0`, `0.5`, `10`, `-3.25`. Refused as `invalid_number`: `01`, `00`, `.5`, `5.`, `-`, `+1`, empty, exponents, spaces, non-ASCII digits, non-strings.
- Codes are flat (`TAGUCH1540:number_too_long`, `invalid_number`, `number_precision`, `number_range`, no `:responses` suffix) and never echo the value. Order: raw length above 80 gives `number_too_long` first, before any regex or copy; bad shape gives `invalid_number`; more than 64 significant digits (counted from the first nonzero digit, trailing zeros counted) or more than 64 fraction digits gives `number_precision`; absolute value above 10^12 gives `number_range`. The bound is inclusive: `1000000000000` is accepted, `1000000000000.0000000001` is refused.
- Consequences I found while building it: no 80-character string with a valid shape is accepted: I computed the longest accepted string by enumerating sign, whole-digit count and fraction-digit count with the least-significant digits (a test pins it) and it is 67 characters (`-0.` plus 64 zeros). Every valid-shape 80-character string is refused as `number_precision`; `number_range` is reached only by shorter strings. The length cap of 80 is therefore reachable only as the first refusal or as `number_precision`. The earlier "80 characters with leading zeros accepted" pin was removed because leading zeros are now illegal. The earlier 64-significant-digit exact-parse pins now use at most 12 whole digits plus a fraction, since the range bound refuses larger values.
- Output words: no generated output key or string value (case-folded, split on non-alphanumeric characters including underscore) has a token that equals or starts with best, better, optimal, optimum, recommend, rank, winner, winning, robust, improve, superior, ideal or preferred. Caller text (run ids, factor names) is excluded. The boundary and array texts were reworded to comply (they said "ranking" and "robustness"). One exempt output key: `robustness_certified` (a boolean disclaimer, value False), pinned in the tests; it is the only key that carries a banned prefix. This keeps the earlier output field; removing it instead is an option for the peer to choose.
- The phrase `declared array, not matched to any catalogue` is present in `array_note` and `boundary` (pinned).
- Mean arithmetic: the level means are computed in `_mean`, inside the same decimal-exception catch as the signal-to-noise values; any decimal signal gives `TAGUCH1540:arithmetic` with no echo. A mean that exceeds the quantization precision cannot be produced from inputs bounded by 10^12 (the signal-to-noise values are small), so the pin forces it with a context subclass whose second `divide` returns 1E+80.

## Child update: counted number witnesses (peer-stated, relayed; counted here with a tool)
- Counted by running `_value`: longest accepted string is 67 characters (`-0.` + 63 zeros + `1`); 65-character accepts are `999999999999.` + 52 nines and `1000000000000.` + 51 zeros; `999999999999.` + 53 nines (66) and `0.` + 64 zeros + `1` (67) are `number_precision`; `1.` + 78 zeros (80) is `number_precision`; `1.` + 79 zeros and 81 zeros (81) are `number_too_long`; 80 zeros is `invalid_number`; `1000000000000.0000000001` is `number_range`.
